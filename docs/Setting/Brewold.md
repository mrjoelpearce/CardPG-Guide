Each of you has found yourself in Brewold, a small town nestled between the wastes and the Western Woods.

Like the rest of [Veleris Coast](Veleris%20Coast.md), Brewold has its share of troubles, but it is safer and quieter than most of the surrounding communities. All of you have been staying at **The Blue Goblet**, the town's sole public house and inn, but you will need to find work if you want to stay.

Brewold's main store, **Drog's Supplies and Sundries**, has a selection of adventuring items for sale, but is struggling to support the various encampments that have started popping up around the town's borders. Because of that lack of stock, most people who land in Brewold end up heading North to [Ragwater](Ragwater.md) or South to [Everfield](Everfield.md)
